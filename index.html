Interactive exhibit

Bring the marker to life.

Point your phone camera at the laptop screen to trigger the 3D scene. The Hiro marker unlocks a playful AR display with motion, colour, and interactive objects.

  • Animated geometry with floating motion and rotation
  • Tap interactions for colour and scale changes
  • Real-time detection banner when the marker is found
