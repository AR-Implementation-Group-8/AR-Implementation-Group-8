Interactive exhibit

Hiro marker experience

Point your phone camera at the laptop display to trigger the AR scene. The marker activates the exhibit and reveals an animated set of objects and interactions.

  • Animated geometry with rotation and floating motion
  • Tap-to-change sphere colour and cylinder size
  • Live marker detection banner for found and lost states
